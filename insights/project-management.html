Gomaae 胡麻和え

A desktop project-management app built around HOU REN SOU (報告・連絡・相談) — the Japanese practice of structured reporting, communication, and consultation — with AI agents acting as the sesame dressing that binds it together.

Philosophy

Why HOU REN SOU?

Most project-management tools optimize for ticket throughput. Gomaae starts from a different premise: teams fail less from a lack of tools and more from a lack of structured communication. HOU REN SOU — 報告 (report), 連絡 (inform), 相談 (consult) — is a decades-old Japanese business discipline for keeping everyone aligned without constant meetings. Gomaae builds that discipline directly into the software: every ticket, brainstorm, and release carries its own reporting and consultation trail, with AI agents doing the busywork of summarizing, scoring, and drafting so the humans can focus on judgment calls.

報告
Houkoku — Report

Status flows upward automatically — AI-drafted release notes and initiative scores keep stakeholders current without status-meeting overhead.

連絡
Renraku — Inform

Everyone touching a ticket sees the same git graph, Linear sync, and activity trail — no information silos between engineering and product.

相談
Soudan — Consult

The Brainstorm sandbox and AI initiative scoring turn "should we build this?" into a structured conversation instead of a gut call.

What It Does
🗂️

Ticket Hierarchy

Epics → Stories → Tasks → Tests, with Kanban and Gantt views over the same underlying data.

🎯

Initiative Scoring

AI-evaluated strategy across 11 product pillars — Vision, Market, UX, Technology, Business Model, Data, Team, Compliance, Go-to-Market, Impact, Execution Risk.

💡

Brainstorm Sandbox

Napkin-sketch ideas that automatically flow into the Epic backlog once they clear initiative scoring.

🚀

Release Management

AI-written release notes, structured feedback collection, and auto-created bug/feature tickets from user reports.

🌿

Branch Activity

A live git graph for the linked repository, so implementation status is never a guess.

🔄

Linear Sync

Bi-directional ticket sync via a dual-id model — local id and Linear external_id stay in lockstep.
