Stores are real shop locations from OpenStreetMap (queried via the Overpass API) — every one has a real street address and coordinates, not an estimated centroid. Coverage is limited to shops mapped with a complete address inside the query's bounding box (core Vancouver / Burnaby / Richmond / North Shore), so it undercounts the true regional total.
H3 resolution follows map zoom: Res 11 fully zoomed in, each resolution held for 2 zoom steps while zooming out (11, 11, 10, 10, 9, 9, …) down to Res 1. The dropdown jumps the map to a preset resolution's zoom.
