Hands-Free Voice-Only Telegram

No screen, no typing. TeleTalk reads incoming Telegram messages aloud and turns your spoken replies into text — started, stopped, and sent with a single click of your headset's play/pause button.

How It Works

Built for moments when your hands and eyes are busy — driving, cooking, walking — but the conversation can't wait.

One-Click Control

Click your headset's play/pause button once to start listening, once more to send. The on-screen mic button does the same if you're not wearing headphones.

Automatic Read-Aloud

Incoming messages are spoken the moment they arrive, in a voice matched to the message's own language, so you never have to glance at the screen.

Live Speech-to-Text

Apple's on-device Speech framework transcribes what you say in real time, with automatic punctuation, as you're speaking.

On-Device Cleanup

Where Apple Intelligence is available, filler words and false starts are quietly cleaned up before sending — entirely on your device, never sent to us.

Pocket Mode

Lock the on-screen controls, dim the display, and slide the phone into your pocket — the headset button keeps the conversation going.

Multi-Language

Switch which language TeleTalk listens for on a per-conversation basis, independent of your device's system language.

Requirements: a real iOS device (speech recognition and the headset button don't work in the Simulator), microphone & speech recognition permission, and your own Telegram account.
